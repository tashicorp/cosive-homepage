What is FIRST

FIRST is the world’s largest network of incident response teams

The Forum of Incident Response and Security Teams (FIRST) has been connecting security teams since 1990. Today it brings together more than 700 teams across over 100 countries — from national CERTs and government agencies to MSSPs, financial institutions, and technology companies.

FIRST membership signals that your team meets a recognised standard of incident response maturity and trustworthiness. It gives you access to a trusted community where teams share intelligence, coordinate on incidents, and learn from each other’s experience. For many organisations, FIRST membership is a meaningful credential that demonstrates your commitment to professional security operations.

How we help

A structured path from gap analysis to FIRST membership

We take a methodical approach to FIRST membership — starting with a clear picture of where your team stands today, then working with you to close the gaps before you apply. This means your application is well-prepared and your team genuinely benefits from the process, not just the credential.

01

SIM3 gap analysis

We assess your team against the Security Incident Management Maturity Model (SIM3) — the same framework FIRST uses to evaluate applicants. This gives you a clear, structured baseline across organisation, human, tools, and processes so you know exactly where you stand and what needs attention.

02

Process improvement

Based on the gap analysis, we work with your team to address the areas that need improvement. This might include documenting incident response procedures, clarifying team roles, improving tooling, or strengthening your information-sharing practices. We focus on practical changes that improve your operations — not just ticking boxes.

03

Readiness assessment

Once improvements are in place, we run a second SIM3 assessment to confirm your team is ready to apply. This gives you confidence that your application will stand up to scrutiny and that your team genuinely meets the maturity standards FIRST expects.

04

FIRST application support

We help you prepare and submit your FIRST membership application, drawing on our experience with the process and our relationships within the FIRST community. We guide you through the application requirements, help you present your team’s capabilities clearly, and support you through to acceptance.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about FIRST membership

What is FIRST?

FIRST (Forum of Incident Response and Security Teams) is the world’s largest community of incident response teams. Founded in 1990, it connects more than 700 teams across over 100 countries — national CERTs, government agencies, financial institutions, technology companies and MSSPs — who share threat intelligence, coordinate incident response and develop the standards used across the industry. It also runs the annual FIRST Conference, one of the most respected defender events globally.

Why should we join FIRST?

FIRST membership gives your incident-response team access to trusted intelligence-sharing channels — including the FIRST MISP server — direct collaboration with experienced teams worldwide, discounted access to the annual FIRST Conference, and participation in the Special Interest Groups that shape industry standards. It’s also a recognised credential that signals to regulators, partners and customers that your team meets an international standard of maturity.

What does the FIRST application process involve?

Applying for FIRST membership involves a structured maturity assessment against specific criteria, a member sponsor, and documentation of your team’s processes. Cosive runs a SIM3 gap analysis upfront to benchmark you against those requirements, close the gaps and prepare the application — so you go in ready rather than discovering issues mid-review.

Can we apply for FIRST membership ourselves?

You can — and some teams succeed on their own. But applications are judged against maturity criteria that aren’t always obvious from the outside, so unprepared teams often find it takes longer or surfaces gaps they didn’t expect. Working with Cosive means you go in knowing what evaluators look for, and the upfront SIM3 gap analysis leaves your team stronger before you even submit.
