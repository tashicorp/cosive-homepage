About Cosive

We help organisations share threat intelligence and fraud data. We have done it more than 100 times.

Since 2015, we have worked with financial services, government, critical infrastructure, and enterprise teams to start, improve, and run intelligence and data sharing programmes.

What we do

CTI

Cyber Threat Intelligence

Community

We contribute to the security and fraud communities, not just consume from them

01

CTI-CMM contributors

Co-authors of the Cyber Threat Intelligence Capability Maturity Model, the framework used by security teams globally to benchmark and improve their CTI programmes.

02

Fraud data sharing programme designers

Designers and builders of national and regional fraud data sharing programmes connecting banks, payment providers, and regulators.

03

Open source contributors

Active contributors to MISP, the open-source platform used for both threat intelligence and fraud data sharing, and participants in OASIS STIX and TAXII standardisation.

04

FIRST committee members

Members of FIRST.org committees and SIGs that shape how CSIRTs and security teams share intelligence across borders.

05

Sharing community architects

Designed and built sharing communities for NCSCs, financial sector ISACs, fraud consortiums, and public-private partnerships across four continents.

06

International speakers and trainers

Regular speakers at FIRST, NZITF, AusCERT, and conferences worldwide. Deliver hands-on training on threat intelligence and fraud data sharing to teams across multiple continents.
