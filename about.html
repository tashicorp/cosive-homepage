Giving back

We believe security works best when people share what they know. That's why we invest time in the communities, standards, and open source projects that make threat intelligence more accessible and more effective for everyone.

Our team speaks at conferences, contributes to international frameworks, and helps build the tools and sharing programmes that security teams around the world rely on every day.

We speak at international conferences

Regular speakers at FIRST, AusCERT, hack.lu, CyberCon, NZITF, CyberUK, and other international security events. We deliver MISP training and workshops on CTI and fraud data sharing to audiences worldwide.

We donate time to open source projects

Active contributors to the MISP open source threat intelligence platform, helping build tools used by thousands of organisations globally.

We helped make CTI-CMM

Contributors to the Cyber Threat Intelligence Capability Maturity Model (CTI-CMM), an internationally recognised framework for assessing and improving CTI programmes.

We helped develop STIX 2.x

Founding members of the OASIS CTI Technical Committee who helped develop the STIX, TAXII, and CybOX international threat intelligence standards.

We help build national sharing programmes

Co-designers of national threat intelligence sharing platforms, helping governments establish infrastructure for cross-sector intelligence sharing.

We volunteer for FIRST SIGs

Members of FIRST.org Special Interest Groups, contributing to global initiatives that advance incident response and threat intelligence practices worldwide.

We volunteer for security communities

Active participants in international security communities including the New Zealand Internet Taskforce (NZITF), coordinating cyber response and intelligence sharing across borders.
