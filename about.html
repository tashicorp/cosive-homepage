Community

We’re passionate about giving back to the security community

International speakers & trainers

Regular speakers and keynotes at FIRST, AusCERT, hack.lu, CyberCon, NZITF, and CyberUK. We deliver hands-on MISP training at AusCERT and hack.lu, and run CTI and fraud data sharing workshops worldwide.

Open source contributors

Active contributors to MISP, the open-source platform used for both threat intelligence and fraud data sharing, and participants in OASIS STIX and TAXII standardisation.

CTI-CMM contributors

Contributors to the Cyber Threat Intelligence Capability Maturity Model, the framework used by security teams globally to benchmark and improve their CTI programmes.

National sharing community co-designers

Co-designers of CTIS, Australia’s national Cyber Threat Intelligence Sharing platform serving 450+ organisations.

FIRST committee members

Members of FIRST.org committees and SIGs that shape how CSIRTs and security teams share intelligence across borders.

NZITF committee members

Our COO chairs the New Zealand Internet Taskforce, the cross-sector group that coordinates New Zealand’s response to cyber security threats and fosters intelligence sharing across government and industry.

Our people

Work directly with leading experts in the field

Terry MacDonald

Principal Security Consultant & COO

Chairperson of the New Zealand Internet Taskforce (NZITF) and co-chair of FIRST IEP-SIG. 25+ years of information security expertise. Founding member of the OASIS CTI Technical Committee who helped develop the STIX, TAXII, and CybOX threat intelligence standards.

Chris Horsley

Principal Security Consultant & CTO

Internationally acclaimed speaker and trainer on cyber threat intelligence and fraud data sharing. Core contributor to the MISP threat intelligence platform and to CTI-CMM, the leading CTI maturity framework. Prior to founding Cosive in 2015, Chris spent 12 years working with national CSIRTs.

Prescott Pym

Principal Security Consultant

Co-designed Australia's national Cyber Threat Intelligence Sharing platform, CTIS. Contributor to CTI-CMM, the leading CTI maturity framework. More than 25 years of expertise managing Security Operations Centers and Cyber Threat Intelligence initiatives across Australia, India, Japan, Germany, Switzerland, and the USA.

Plus a team of specialists

Behind our principals are senior security consultants and security engineers — each with deep expertise in threat intelligence platforms, MISP deployments, and sharing infrastructure. Every engagement is staffed by experienced practitioners, never juniors.
