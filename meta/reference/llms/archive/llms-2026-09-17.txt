# Cosive

> Cosive helps security teams operationalise threat intelligence and fraud data sharing through managed platforms, integrations, and expert consulting.

Cosive is a cybersecurity consultancy founded in 2015 that specialises in cyber threat intelligence (CTI), security operations (SecOps), and fraud data sharing. We help organisations consume, share, and act on threat intelligence and fraud data through our managed MISP platform (CloudMISP), custom tool development, and expert consulting.

## Services

### CloudMISP
Managed MISP hosting for CTI teams. Three bundles — Core, Accelerator, and Sharing — give your team a threat intelligence platform without the infrastructure overhead.
- URL: https://www.cosive.com/cloudmisp

### Cyber Threat Intelligence
CTI programme consulting, MISP deployment, sharing communities, and tool integration. We help organisations consume, share, and act on cyber threat intelligence.
- URL: https://www.cosive.com/cyber-threat-intelligence

### Security Operations
SOC maturity assessments, CSIRT setup, SOAR automation, and FIRST membership guidance. We help you build, run, and improve security operations.
- URL: https://www.cosive.com/security-operations

### Fraud Data Sharing
We help banks and financial institutions share fraud data securely using MISP. Platform deployment, community design, and fraud tool integration.
- URL: https://www.cosive.com/fraud-data-sharing
