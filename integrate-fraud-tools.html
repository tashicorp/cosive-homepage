Integrate your fraud tools

Connecting fraud systems together is how you turn intelligence into action. We help you link your fraud management tools, sharing platforms, and regulatory connections — so your team spends less time on manual work and more time stopping fraud.

How it works

MISP is at the heart of fraud sharing — we help you connect to it

Most fraud data sharing communities use MISP as their core platform. We’re MISP experts — we help you develop integrations between your fraud management platforms and MISP so fraud indicators flow automatically into your detection and response workflows.

We work with what you have. Whether it’s your transaction monitoring system, fraud case management tool, or SIEM — if it has an API, we can connect it to your MISP instance and the communities you belong to.

FAQ

Questions we hear about fraud tool integration

Can you help me get MISP fraud data into my fraud platform?

Yes — we build integrations that pull fraud indicators from MISP into your existing fraud management tools. Whether that’s a direct API connection, a scheduled sync, or a custom plugin, we connect the platforms so your fraud team gets actionable data without manual steps.

What fraud data sharing platforms do you support?

We work with MISP-based platforms (including our own CloudMISP), STIX/TAXII platforms, custom APIs, and proprietary formats. If your regulator or peers use a specific platform, we can connect to it — or help you stand up your own.

My regulator’s platform isn’t listed. Can you help me connect to my regulator?

Yes. We adapt to whatever platform or format your regulator requires. We’ve built integrations for regulators across Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific, and we’re used to working with new systems and specifications. If you can share the technical requirements, we can build the connection.

Can you help me start a fraud data sharing community?

Yes. We help with community design, membership rules, workflows, and the technical platform. We’ve done this for central banks and industry groups. See our sharing community page for more detail.

Can you also help me share cyber threat intelligence?

Yes. The same platform that supports fraud data sharing also handles cyber threat intelligence. If you need to share both fraud indicators and CTI, we can set that up on a single platform rather than running two separate programmes. Learn more about our CTI work.
